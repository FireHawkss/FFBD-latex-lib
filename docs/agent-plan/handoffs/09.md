## Result

The exported `solve(Spec, Metrics, Frame) -> Scene | nil, Diagnostic[]` API and
64-attempt search budget remain unchanged. Attempts are shared across structural
plans, and sampled note combinations cover more than the first placement prefix.
Note candidates prioritize explicit and global preferences. Labels can slide
along both sides of segments, including short segments, and avoid every segment
of their own route, other paths, nodes, notes and measured group captions.

Measured label allowances now influence primary and cross-band spacing. Parallel
row-height estimates include every lane. The planner penalizes splitting groups
and prunes oversized multipage rows before beam trimming. Group rectangles reserve
space for owned notes and measured captions. Complete Scenes are copied and
translated uniformly to a positive page origin before the final bounds check;
scale never changes. Same-row reverse movement is penalized, while a legitimate
serpentine transition is not. Extra wraps and an unforced singleton first row add
to the grouping score. Continuations use reader-facing numeric labels and can
cross a page inside one semantic structure without inventing semantic group ports.

The seven quality components remain hard violations; wrong-way distance; crossings
plus congestion; strong costs plus placement crossings plus row deviation; gap
variance plus blank area plus page-area variance plus weak costs; bends weighted
by route gap plus route length; and displaced preferred notes. These remain
heuristic weights, not a completed empirical calibration. Router work is recorded
for all attempted routes and the selected candidate.
